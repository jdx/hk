# Bound for the Main

_A sea shanty about the hk git hook manager_

<ShantyVideo />

The lyrics are below. For the tool itself, start with [getting started](/getting_started).

## Verse 1

> I christened her "feat: hoist the sails", _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> And I typed git commit and hit return,<br>
> Then the hook piped all hands on deck, _(Haul away!)_<br>
> And they tumbled up from stem to stern!

## Chorus

> **Heave away, haul away, hk!** _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> **All hands haul at once!** _(Haul away!)_<br>
> **For a lock on each file takes the strain,**<br>
> **And we're bound away for the main!**

## Verse 2

> I'd a TODO line I hadn't staged, _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> So hk stashed it safe from the cold,<br>
> For the crew see just the cargo I ship, _(Haul away!)_<br>
> And my line lay snug in the hold.

## Verse 3

> Then shfmt, prettier and ruff, _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> All tailed on at once on the rising tide,<br>
> Each hand on a file of their own, made fast, _(Haul away!)_<br>
> So the three of them hauled side by side!

_(Chorus)_

## Verse 4

> All the fixes were staged, so I skipped the dance, _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> No "fail, git add, and commit again",<br>
> Then my stash came up like an anchor weighed, _(Haul away!)_<br>
> And "feat: hoist the sails" made the main!

_(Chorus)_

## Bridge

> Who'll check the cargo? _(hk check!)_<br>
> Who'll mend the canvas? _(hk fix!)_<br>
> Who'll haul the halyard? _(All hands at once!)_<br>
> And where are we bound? _(Bound for the main!)_

## Verse 5

> The hook, the helm and the harbor-master, _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> All muster the self-same crew,<br>
> For they steer by the one set of charts, _(Haul away!)_<br>
> In hk.pkl, typed and true!

## Verse 6

> Her sister sailed with deploy.sh, _(Bound for the main?)_<br>
> And a loose end no fixer could splice,<br>
> Then shellcheck sang out from aloft, "Bug, ho!" _(Where away?)_<br>
> "Dead ahead!" And the hook tugged her twice!<br>
> _(Heave!)_<br>
> _(Ho!)_

## Final Chorus

> **Heave away, haul away, hk!** _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> **All hands haul at once!** _(Haul away!)_<br>
> **For a lock on each file takes the strain,**<br>
> **But she never made the main!**

## Outro

> So all you hands who would sign aboard, _(Heave ho!)_<br>
> There's but three words left to say:<br>
> mise use hk! _(mise use hk!)_<br>
> And we're bound away for the main!
